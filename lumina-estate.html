Tricia's Therapies

Holistic Wellness & Nursing

View Live Site

The challenge

Tricia’s Therapies, led by a former Clinical Nurse Manager and Director of Nursing, offered a unique blend of medical knowledge and energetic healing. However, the brand’s initial digital presence struggled to communicate this professional duality.

The primary obstacles included:

  • The "Clinical vs. Spiritual" Gap: The brand needed to appeal to people seeking alternative healing (Reiki, Rahanni) without losing the trust earned from Tricia’s 18 years of high-level nursing experience.
  • Service Navigation: With diverse offerings ranging from Psychotherapy to Integrated Energy Therapy, users were often unsure which path was right for their specific needs.
  • Manual Booking Friction: The absence of a streamlined inquiry and educational flow meant much of the practitioner's time was spent answering basic procedural questions rather than focusing on client care.

The solution

I developed a clean, empathetic, and professional web experience that prioritizes "The Path to Healing." The design focuses on soft aesthetics backed by a clear, authoritative structure.

Strategic Implementations:

  • The Trust-First Bio Architecture: I positioned Tricia’s extensive nursing background (Dementia and Palliative care) as the foundation of the site. This immediately established professional credibility before introducing the holistic services.
  • Categorized Service Modalities: I organized the diverse treatments into distinct "pillars of care." This allowed users to self-identify their needs—whether they were seeking mental health support through Counseling or energetic alignment through Rahanni.
  • Education-Led Conversion: Instead of a hard-sell approach, I implemented an "Informed Healing" strategy. Each service page was designed to explain the "what" and the "why," lowering the barrier to entry for users new to energy healing.
  • Integrated Community Growth: I prioritized a high-visibility newsletter integration and community-focused CTAs, transforming the site from a static business card into a growing wellness ecosystem.

The results

The digital transformation of Tricia’s Therapies successfully aligned the online experience with the physical quality of the sessions provided.

  • Significant Increase in New Client Inquiries: The clear categorization of services led to a higher volume of targeted inquiries from clients who felt they truly understood the offerings.
  • High Engagement on Educational Content: Average time spent on service-specific pages increased, indicating that users were utilizing the site as a resource for their wellness journey.
  • Streamlined Brand Messaging: By successfully merging clinical experience with holistic practice, the brand saw a rise in referrals from both medical professionals and the spiritual community.
  • Mobile-Optimized Accessibility: The responsive layout ensured that clients in high-stress situations could easily access contact information and calming resources from any device.
