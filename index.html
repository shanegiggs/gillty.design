Frequently asked questions.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites are completed within 2-4 weeks, depending on complexity.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! We offer unlimited revisions to ensure your site is perfect.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes monthly instalments can be arranged or you can split payment into 3-4 payments.

Can I request changes to my website after it's live?

We offer unlimited help and revisions up until launch.

Once launched we offer 3 weeks post launch support in case anything goes wrong however, this does not include any major changes to your site. If you would like to receive monthly support then this can be added on upon purchase.

Does your package come with hosting?

Hosting does not come with the labeled packages above however this can be added as an add-on upon purchase. Hosting starts at €30 euro a month depending on your needs.
