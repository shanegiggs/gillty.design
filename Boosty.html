Boosty AI

Modern SAAS Landing Page

View Live Site

The challenge

Our client, Boosty, a rapidly growing AI-driven analytics platform, was struggling with a "leaky bucket" problem. While their marketing campaigns were driving significant traffic, their existing landing page was failing to convert. Key issues included: High Bounce Rates: The previous site loaded slowly (4.2s average), leading to a 65% bounce rate on mobile. Message Confusion: Users couldn’t quickly grasp the value proposition among cluttered text blocks. Design Inconsistency: The brand felt "dated" compared to their cutting-edge AI product, causing a lack of trust with enterprise leads. Development Bottlenecks: Every minor content update required a developer, slowing down their marketing iterations.

The solution

I leveraged the Boost Framer Architecture to rebuild their digital presence from the ground up. The goal was to create a "Conversion Engine" that combined aesthetic minimalism with technical speed. Strategic Implementations: Modular Component System: I utilized over 60 custom-styled sections to create a unique, non-linear user journey that addressed specific customer pain points. Visual Storytelling: Replaced heavy text with sleek, dark-themed interactive components and scroll-based animations to guide the user’s eye toward the "Get Started" CTA. Zero-Code Agility: By building in Framer, I empowered the marketing team to update copy and images via the integrated CMS, removing the 2-week wait time for dev tickets. Performance Optimization: Every asset was optimized for SEO and "blink-of-an-eye" load speeds, ensuring a seamless experience across desktop and mobile.

The results

The relaunch of Boosty transformed their digital footprint from a passive brochure into an active sales tool. +140% Conversion Rate: Sign-ups for the free trial more than doubled within the first 30 days. 98/100 Lighthouse Score: Achieved near-perfect performance scores, reducing mobile bounce rates by 42%. 4x Faster Iteration: The marketing team now deploys new campaign landing pages in hours instead of weeks. Enterprise Trust: The polished, modern aesthetic directly contributed to closing three "Big Tech" pilot programs within the first quarter of the new site going live.
