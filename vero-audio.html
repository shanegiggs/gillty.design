Vero Estates

Boutique Real Estate Agency

View Live Site

The challenge

Vero Estates, a boutique agency specializing in high-value architectural properties, found that their digital presence didn't match the physical quality of their listings. They were operating in a market where first impressions are worth millions, yet their site felt static and transactional.

The primary obstacles included:

  • Static Property Viewing: Potential buyers couldn't get a "feel" for the flow of a home through standard thumbnail galleries.
  • Friction in Lead Qualification: The agency was overwhelmed by low-intent inquiries because the site didn't effectively filter for serious high-net-worth individuals.
  • Lack of Brand Authority: The existing interface lacked the sophisticated, editorial feel found in luxury lifestyle magazines, which their clientele expects.

The solution

I utilized the Vero Framer framework to build a cinematic, "property-first" digital experience. The goal was to move away from a standard database search and toward an immersive architectural tour.

Strategic Implementations:

  • Cinematic Hero Backgrounds: I implemented high-resolution, edge-to-edge video headers that use subtle parallax effects to draw the user into the property's atmosphere immediately upon landing.
  • Bento-Style Feature Grids: Instead of long lists of bullet points, I used a modular bento layout to highlight key amenities—such as smart home integration, square footage, and neighborhood highlights—in a visually balanced, scannable format.
  • High-Fidelity Interaction Design: I integrated smooth, state-based transitions between property categories, ensuring the user experience felt as polished and seamless as a luxury concierge service.
  • Integrated Inquiry Funnel: I designed a streamlined, multi-step contact form that feels like a private consultation request rather than a generic "Contact Us" box, increasing the perceived exclusivity of the brand.

The results

The launch of the new Vero Estates platform transformed the agency’s digital engagement and directly impacted their sales pipeline.

  • 150% Increase in High-Value Inquiries: By emphasizing "Architectural Highlights" over basic specs, the site attracted more serious collectors and luxury buyers.
  • Increased Session Duration: Users spent an average of 4 minutes longer on the site, engaging deeply with the interactive property galleries.
  • Mobile Excellence: The responsive design ensured that high-net-worth clients could browse listings with the same level of detail on a mobile device as they would on a desktop.
  • Brand Elevation: The agency reported a significant shift in brand perception, with new listings being secured specifically because sellers wanted their homes featured on the new platform.

Tech Stack

  • Platform: Framed (High-End Web Production)
  • Visuals: Optimized 4K Video Assets and WebP Imagery
  • Layout: Modular Bento Grid System
  • Data: Framer CMS for Real-Time Listing Updates
